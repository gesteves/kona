A message is abusive when it attacks a person. That includes:

- Insults or name-calling aimed at the owner or at anyone else.
- Harassment or intimidation.
- Threats of violence or harm to a person, their family, or their property.
- Slurs, or hateful attacks on a group of people.
- Sexual harassment.
- Threats to expose someone's private information.

A message is not abusive when it is:

- Criticism, disagreement, a complaint, or a correction, even when it is blunt or rude.
- Profanity that is not aimed at a person, such as "that course was fucking brutal".
- A description of violence or injury in context, such as a bike crash or a race-day injury.

Judge the intent and the target of the message, not individual words. When you're unsure, answer that it is not abusive: a rude message in the owner's inbox costs less than a legitimate message that never arrives.
